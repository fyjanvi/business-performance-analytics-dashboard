📊 Global Superstore Power BI Analytics Project

🚀 Overview
This project presents an end-to-end Business Intelligence (BI) analytics solution built using the Global Superstore dataset.
The goal is to deliver actionable insights across Sales, Customer Behavior, Product Performance, and Operations & Shipping.
A complete 4-page interactive Power BI dashboard was created to support executive decision-making and help monitor performance across multiple business functions.
This project mirrors the workflow of real-world analyst roles—BI Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Analyst, Operations Analyst, Product Analyst, Financial Analyst—making it perfect for showcasing industry-ready skills.

📊 Dashboard Pages & Key Insights
1️⃣ Executive Summary Dashboard
This page provides a high-level business performance view.
Key Metrics
Total Sales: 12.63M
Total Profit: 1.47M
Total Orders: 25.04K
Profit Margin (%)
Insights
West region contributes the highest revenue but shows fluctuating profit margins.
Sales peak in October–December, showing strong holiday season demand.
Consumer segment accounts for over 50% of total sales, making it the dominant customer group.
Profitability varies significantly across segments—Furniture has strong revenue but weaker margins.

2️⃣ Sales Dashboard
Analyzes product and category-level performance.
Insights
Technology is the top-performing category in both revenue and profit.
Phones and Copiers lead the sub-categories, contributing major revenue.
Tables and Bookcases show low or negative profit due to higher discounting.
The Central region leads in sales among all regions.

3️⃣ Customer Insights Dashboard
Understanding customer behavior, value, and distribution.
Insights
Top 10 customers contribute a significant portion of sales, indicating strong reliance on high-value accounts.
Corporate and Consumer segments show higher lifetime value compared to Home Office.
Central and West regions host the highest number of customers.
Repeat customers generate higher profit contributions compared to first-time buyers.

4️⃣ Operations & Shipping Dashboard
Evaluating shipping modes, costs, and delivery performance.
Insights
Standard Class accounts for ~60% of all shipments but has the slowest delivery time.
Same Day shipping shows the fastest delivery but represents the smallest share (~5%).
Shipping costs are highest for Technology products followed by Furniture.
Delivery delays increased slightly between 2011–2014, suggesting potential operational inefficiencies.

🎯 Business Recommendations
Sales & Marketing
Reduce discounting on low-profit categories like Furniture and Tables.
Invest in promoting high-growth categories such as Technology.
Strengthen targeted marketing toward the Consumer segment (top revenue driver).

Customer Strategy
Create loyalty programs for high-value customers to improve retention.
Focus on upselling in Corporate and Consumer segments.
Improve customer engagement in slower regions such as Africa and EMEA.

Operations & Logistics
Optimize Standard Class shipping routes to reduce delivery days.
Negotiate better shipping contracts for heavy Furniture items.
Use historical delivery data to forecast and pre-stock in high-demand regions.

Overall Business Strategy
Balance product portfolio by improving margins in Furniture.
Increase focus on Central and West regions where demand is strongest.
Address operational delays to improve customer satisfaction.
